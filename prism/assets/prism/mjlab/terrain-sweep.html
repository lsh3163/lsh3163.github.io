The focused random-rough comparison was chosen after inspecting this seven-terrain sweep. The pyramid profile starts on a central platform and can enter a descending section first; it is not a continuous uphill test. PRISM had 23 early orientation falls in the first descending section of hf_pyramid_slope; survival there was 77/100, versus MLP 97/100 and Matched 100/100.

Training configuration

All methods use the same native-mjlab task and 30,000-update budget. Startup samples combine 20% nominal physics with 80% independent friction 0.3–1.2, link mass/inertia ratio 0.8–1.2 and fixed torso payload 0–5 kg. Shared final-hidden RMSNorm is applied equally to all three actors.
