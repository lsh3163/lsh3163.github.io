All seven native terrain families

All 4,200 episodes and 4,900 actual initial-state comparisons were validated. Each actor was trained once for 30,000 PPO updates; five prescribed terrain/reset groups supply 100 attempts per cell. Evaluation uses nominal friction 0.6, link density ratio 1, payload 0 kg, command 0.5 m/s and 20 seconds from rest. RMSE and forward distance include every attempted episode, including early failures.

Survival requires the complete horizon inside the certified support footprint. The separate compound criterion additionally requires linear and yaw RMSE ≤0.2 and directed progress ≥80% of commanded displacement. Random rough was chosen as a focused comparison after this broad sweep. These within-family terrain results do not establish universal terrain or training-seed robustness.
