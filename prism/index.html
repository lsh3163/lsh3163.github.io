The idea

Give the policy access to interactions.

01 / Method

Robot dynamics depend on products of physical variables. PRISM makes multiplicative interactions among available observations explicit and learnable, instead of asking an MLP to discover every product implicitly.

Two affine branches form a compact polynomial representation. Replace the first affine state projection; keep the remaining backbone, learning objective, observation interface, and low-level controller.

  • Physics-motivated, data-driven. No hand-coded dynamics equations.
  • Compact by construction. Bilinear factors avoid enumerating all polynomial terms.
  • RL and imitation learning. Train with the policy's existing objective.

Evaluation scope & sampling

Humanoid-Gym

Flat-ground trials use the native 60-second timeout. Survival is mean ± sample SD across five evaluation seeds, 200 episodes per seed/condition, at fixed trained checkpoints. The comparison MLP is parameter-matched.

mjlab G1

Random-rough trials use 20-second supported survival at a 0.5 m/s forward command. Each method and condition has 1,000 paired attempts: five fixed layouts × 200 resets. Conditional Wilson 95% intervals describe those checkpoints and layouts. The comparison is the standard MLP.

mjlab training support: friction 0.3–1.2; coherent link mass/inertia multiplier 0.8–1.2; torso payload 0–5 kg. The paper's headline uses six out-of-range physical settings and excludes the extra μ = 0.1 endpoint from that mean.
